# QazaqVocab

QazaqVocab is a modern vocabulary-discovery app for Russian-speaking people learning Kazakh.

## Language

**Learner**:
A Russian-speaking person who wants to discover and learn Kazakh vocabulary.
_Avoid_: User, student

**Vocabulary entry**:
A curated Kazakh word together with a Russian translation and the information needed to understand it accurately.
_Avoid_: Flashcard, item

**Discovery**:
The learner encounters and understands a useful Kazakh word. The first release does not claim that encountering a word means memorizing or mastering it.
_Avoid_: Mastery, memorization

**Vocabulary mix**:
The TestFlight collection contains 10 basic everyday words, 10 useful conversational words, and 10 expressive or culturally meaningful words, mixed together without visible categories or difficulty labels.
_Avoid_: Difficulty level

**Featured daily word**:
The vocabulary entry highlighted for every learner on a given calendar day as the app's recurring daily experience. It appears first even when a learner has seen it before and rotates deterministically through the reviewed pool.
_Avoid_: Word of the day

**Discovery feed**:
The learner's shuffled, unrestricted but finite stream of unseen vocabulary entries beyond the featured daily word. A learner may explore at any pace, and each reviewed entry appears once before collection completion.
_Avoid_: Endless feed, course, lesson

**Saved words**:
The learner's single personal collection of vocabulary entries they want to keep and revisit.
_Avoid_: Favorites, Want to Learn

**Share card**:
A branded visual representation of a vocabulary entry that a learner can share outside QazaqVocab.
_Avoid_: Export, social post

**Written pronunciation**:
A Latin transliteration that helps a Russian-speaking learner pronounce a Kazakh word. Audio pronunciation is outside the first TestFlight release.
_Avoid_: Audio, recording

**Daily reminder**:
A notification, delivered at a time chosen by the learner during setup, that invites them to open the day's featured word.
_Avoid_: Word drop, alert

**Main card**:
The compact presentation of a vocabulary entry: Kazakh word, Russian meaning, one Kazakh example, and that example's Russian translation.
_Avoid_: Flashcard

**Entry details**:
The expanded presentation of a vocabulary entry: Latin transliteration, Russian part of speech, Russian usage explanation, and up to two additional bilingual examples.
_Avoid_: Lesson

**Collection completion**:
The point when the learner swipes beyond the final unseen entry in the finite TestFlight collection. The app congratulates the learner for testing the first version and stops reminders; saved words remain available, while widgets may continue showing the shared featured daily word from the reviewed pool.
_Avoid_: Reset, automatic refill

**TestFlight collection**:
The first reviewed set of 30 vocabulary entries used to validate QazaqVocab with a small invited group. It is not the intended content limit for a public release.
_Avoid_: Full library, public catalog

**First-time setup**:
A two-step introduction that explains the daily-discovery promise, lets the learner choose a reminder time, requests notification permission, and then opens the featured daily word.
_Avoid_: Registration, account setup

**Words**:
The primary app section, opening on the featured daily word and continuing into the discovery feed.
_Avoid_: Home, Profile

**Saved**:
The app section containing the learner's saved words.
_Avoid_: Profile, Favorites

**Content approval**:
Native-speaker acceptance of a vocabulary entry after an AI-assisted Russian draft and linguistic review. Entries are reviewed in groups of five, and only approved entries join the TestFlight collection.
_Avoid_: AI validation, automatic approval

**Visual identity**:
A minimal dark design with restrained Kazakh typography, color, and ornament. The app icon centers an original `Q` symbol with a subtle Kazakh ornamental detail.
_Avoid_: Flag imagery, generic books, copied layouts
